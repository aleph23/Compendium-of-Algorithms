| Architecture | Era | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| [Mixture of Experts (MoE)](/received-canon/modern/mixture-of-experts/) | 1991 / 2022–2024 | Sparse routing activates only K of N expert sub-networks per token — cheap scaling. |
| [State Space Models (Mamba / S4)](/received-canon/modern/state-space-model/) | 2022–2024 | Linear-time sequence modelling via structured state-space equations with selective scan. |
| [Vision Transformer (ViT)](/received-canon/modern/vision-transformer/) | 2020 | Image patches as tokens — pure self-attention replaces convolution for vision. |
| [Multimodal Foundation Models](/received-canon/modern/multimodal-foundation-model/) | 2021–2024 | Unified models bridging text, image, audio and video with shared representations. |
